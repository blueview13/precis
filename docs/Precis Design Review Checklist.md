# Precis Design Review Checklist

## Review goals

Validate that the app reads as Precis, not as a generic reader or dashboard. The summary should feel like a marginal annotation, and the interface should feel quiet and intentional.

## Required review passes

### Light mode
- Confirm the paper/ink palette reads clearly and comfortably.
- Verify sidebars, list rows, and reading panes maintain strong hierarchy.
- Check that summary notes remain visually distinct without overpowering the article.

### Dark mode
- Confirm the dark palette is deliberate rather than a naive inversion.
- Ensure the warm neutral tones remain readable and soft rather than harsh.
- Check that marginalia stays legible and clearly differentiated from body text.

### Window sizes
- Test narrow layouts to confirm the reading pane still feels readable.
- Test wide layouts to confirm the article list and marginal summary do not feel stretched or detached.
- Verify the interface still feels balanced at both compact and expanded window widths.

### Visual identity checks
- The app should not feel like Mail.app, a generic news client, or an AI dashboard.
- Summaries should read as annotations beside the article, not as a sidechat panel.
- The layout should feel reading-first and calm, not crowded or high-contrast.

## Design baseline to keep

- Quiet, deliberate interface with generous spacing
- Strong reading hierarchy and typography-first composition
- Visible but restrained marginalia summary treatment
- Warm neutral palette with dark-mode counterpart
- No visual chrome that competes with the article experience

## Capture artifacts

- one screenshot in light mode
- one screenshot in dark mode
- one screenshot at narrow width
- one screenshot at wide width
- notes on whether the marginalia treatment is legible and distinct

## Acceptance gate

The interface is approved only when the summary annotation feels unmistakably like Precis and the overall design remains distinctly different from generic reader UI.
