# Right-Click Menus

## Feeds

Right-click a feed to refresh or rename it, create a newspaper PDF of its articles from the last 24 hours, move it to a category, use "Remove Feed…" to remove it from its category without deleting it, or delete it with its articles. With multiple feeds selected, move or delete the whole selection.

For Feedbin feeds, manage tags or unsubscribe from Feedbin. Unsubscribing leaves the feed and its articles in Precis.

## Categories

Right-click a category to rename it, change its text and icon color, or create a newspaper PDF. **Remove All Feeds in Category** moves its feeds to No Category without deleting them. You can also delete all feeds inside it or delete the category while keeping its feeds.

## Smart Categories

Right-click a Smart Category to edit its rules, rename it, change its color, create a newspaper PDF, or delete it. Right-click a feed inside it and choose "Remove Feed…" to exclude that feed without deleting it. Deleting a Smart Category does not delete feeds.

The headline list and reading pane have no right-click menus; use their header buttons.
