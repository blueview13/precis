# Getting Started

## Add your first feed

1. Click **Add Feeds** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Paste a URL — RSS feeds work, plus YouTube, Reddit, Google News, X, Bluesky, GitHub, TikTok and more. A URL you just copied is offered automatically.
3. Press **Return**, or click **Add to Precis**. The sheet stays open so you can add several feeds in a row.
4. Nothing to paste? Use **Browse by category…** under *Suggested Feeds* to pick a popular feed.
5. Click **Done** when you're finished.

## Organise feeds into categories

- Click the **+** next to the Categories heading and choose **New Category**.
- Drag a feed onto a category, or right-click the feed and choose **Move to Category**.
- Each feed belongs to at most one category. Feeds with no category stay in the sidebar under their own names.
- Right-click a category to rename it, give it a colour, or delete it (its feeds are kept).

## Read and mark articles

1. Click a headline to open the article — opening it marks it as read.
2. Move through the list with **Space** (next article) or the **↑ / ↓** arrow keys.
3. Press **d** to flip read/unread, **s** to star or unstar.
4. Use the ticks in the headline list's header bar to mark everything visible as read or unread.

Press **⌘?** at any time to reopen this Help window.
