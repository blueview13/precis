# Getting Started

## Add feeds

1. Click **Add Feeds** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Paste a feed or supported site URL. Precis offers a URL copied to the clipboard.
3. Choose **Add to Precis**, **Feedbin**, or **Both** when a Feedbin account is connected, then press **Return** or click the add button. The sheet stays open for more feeds.
4. Choose **Browse by category…** to find suggested feeds, then click **Done**.

## Organise feeds

Use the **+** beside **Categories** to make a category or Smart Category. Drag feeds onto a category, or use a feed's right-click menu. A feed belongs to one regular category; uncategorised feeds remain in the sidebar. Smart Categories collect articles that match the rules you set. Expand or collapse a heading with its chevron. Right-click a category to rename, recolour, or delete it. Deleting a regular category keeps its feeds.

## Read articles

Click a headline to open it and mark it read. Use **Space** for the next article or **↑ / ↓** to move through headlines. Press **d** to toggle read status and **s** to toggle its star. Click a related-story strip to expand its coverage. In the reading pane, use **Read Aloud** or **Translate** when available. Header buttons can mark visible articles read or unread. See **Settings & Languages** for reading, summaries, accounts, and interface options.

Press **⌘?** to open Help at any time.

The Help menu includes **Precis Help**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Support**.
