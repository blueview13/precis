# Getting Started

## Add feeds

1. Click **Add Feeds** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Paste a feed or supported site URL. Precis offers a URL copied to the clipboard.
3. Press **Return** or choose **Add to Precis**. The sheet stays open for more feeds.
4. Choose **Browse by category…** to find suggested feeds, then click **Done**.

## Organise feeds

Use the **+** beside **Categories** to make a category. Drag feeds onto it, or use a feed's right-click menu. A feed belongs to one category; uncategorised feeds remain in the sidebar. Right-click a category to rename, recolour, or delete it. Deleting a category keeps its feeds.

## Read articles

Click a headline to open it and mark it read. Use **Space** for the next article or **↑ / ↓** to move through headlines. Press **d** to toggle read status and **s** to toggle its star. Header buttons can mark the visible articles read or unread. See **Settings & Languages** for reading, summaries, accounts, and interface options.

Press **⌘?** to open Help at any time.
