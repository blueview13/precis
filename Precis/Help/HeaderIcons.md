# Header Icons

The icons at the right of the headline list's header bar, left to right:

- {{icon:newspaper}} — *Create a newspaper PDF*: makes a PDF of this feed or category's articles from the last 24 hours and asks where to save it. Only shown when a feed, category, or Smart Category is selected.
- {{icon:checkmark.circle}} — *Mark visible articles as read*: marks every article currently in the list as read.
- {{icon:envelope.badge}} — *Mark visible articles as unread*: marks every article currently in the list as unread.
- {{icon:square.and.arrow.up}} — *Share article*: opens the system share menu (Mail, Messages, Copy Link, …) for the article open in the reading pane. Only shown when that article has a link.

The two mark-all buttons only appear when there's something to change — the tick when the list has unread articles, the envelope when it has read ones.
