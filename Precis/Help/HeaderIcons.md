# Header Icons

The icons at the right of the headline-list header, from left to right:

- {{icon:newspaper}} — Create a newspaper PDF of the selected feed, category, or Smart Category's articles from the last 24 hours.
- {{icon:checkmark.circle}} — Mark the visible articles as read.
- {{icon:envelope.badge}} — Mark the visible articles as unread.
- {{icon:square.and.arrow.up}} — Open the system share menu for the article in the reading pane. This appears when the article has a link.

The read and unread buttons appear only when there are articles in that state to change.
