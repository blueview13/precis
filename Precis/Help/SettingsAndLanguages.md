# Settings & Languages

Open Settings with **⌘,**. Your interface-language choice is saved and applied to the main window, Settings, sidebar tooltips, and Help. English is the default. Other choices are German, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Bengali, and Portuguese. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Feed names, category names, article text, and other source content are not translated.

## Appearance

Choose a theme and interface language. Theme changes apply to the reader and Settings.

## Reading

- Choose which browser opens article links.
- Choose a macOS voice for **Read Aloud**.
- Choose a target language for **Translate Article**. macOS may download language models the first time you use translation.
- Set the article font, reading-pane font size and width, and whether to show reading-time estimates.

## Article List

Turn on **Stack related stories** to group matching stories among the 800 newest articles. Choose whether to show thumbnails and article images, and set the default sort order.

## Left Sidebar and AI Summaries

Choose whether feed rows show unread-count badges. Control whether Precis automatically generates a summary when you open an article.

## Refresh and Accounts

Choose a background refresh interval. Connect Feedbin or The Old Reader to sync your subscriptions; Feedbin tags are available in the sidebar. Use the OPML section to import or export subscriptions.

## Notifications

Turn on new-article notifications. macOS may ask you to allow notifications for Precis in System Settings.

## Side Panel

Enable **Show menu bar headlines** to show a compact panel at the left or right screen edge. Choose its background and text colors, opacity, and the number of articles to show. Select the feeds or Smart Categories to include, then drag the panel's resize edge to adjust its width.

## OPML

Import an OPML file to add subscriptions, or export your feeds to back them up or use them in another reader.
